In this module, we will introduce the core components of Logical Volume Management, or LVM, in Red Hat Enterprise Linux. LVM provides a flexible approach to storage management by abstracting physical hardware into manageable logical units.

To understand how LVM works, we need to look at two primary building blocks:

First, we have Physical Volumes, or PVs. These are your underlying storage devices, such as raw disks or specific disk partitions, that have been initialized for use by the LVM system.

Second, we have Volume Groups, or VGs. A Volume Group acts as a centralized storage pool, created by combining one or more Physical Volumes. Essentially, the Volume Group serves as the container from which you can allocate your logical volumes.
